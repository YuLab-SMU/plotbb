# plotbb 0.4.0 layer decisions

This note records the decisions behind the new layers in 0.4.0. They follow the
0.2.5 layer contract and the 0.3.0 scale/legend rules.

## bb_ribbon()

* Maps `x`, `ymin`, `ymax`; `fill` (or `col`) sets the interior and can be a
  constant or a mapping. It is the only new layer with an interior, so `fill`
  is allowed by the fill guard.
* Grouping: an explicit `group` mapping wins, otherwise a mapped `fill`
  groups; without either there is one band. This matches the grouping rule the
  statistical layers use.
* Validity: a row is drawn only when `x`, `ymin` and `ymax` are finite; a
  group needs at least two rows. Points are sorted by `x` before the polygon is
  built, so unsorted data cannot self-intersect.
* Constants: `alpha` (0.3, validated to 0-1), `border` (none) and `lty`/`lwd`
  are layer-wide single values passed to `graphics::polygon`; a length-n
  `fill`/`col` constant follows the surviving rows like every other layer.
  A group with fewer than two finite points is skipped with a warning, and a
  missing group warns before its rows are dropped.
* Ranges: `x`, `ymin` and `ymax` all contribute, so a band can extend the
  canvas on its own.

## bb_hline() / bb_vline()

* The position is either a constant vector (`yintercept` / `xintercept`) or a
  mapping of the same name. A mapping wins when both are supplied, which keeps
  one rule: data-driven positions are explicit mappings.
* The layers do not require data when the intercept is constant, so a
  reference line can be added to an `as.bbplot()` canvas. A mapped intercept
  requires data and says so.
* Ranges: a reference line does not change the axis range by default. This is
  deliberate: a line at the edge of the data must not silently stretch the
  canvas. `expand_limits = TRUE` is the explicit opt-in.
* A mapped intercept can carry `col` and `lty` per line, aligned to the
  surviving intercepts; constants are passed through unchanged.
* Facets: the lines are drawn once per panel with the panel's own coordinate
  system, and `expand_limits = TRUE` contributes to every panel's range.

## Not in this release

`bb_violin()` and `bb_smooth()` remain candidates: both need a policy for
bandwidth / fit method and small-sample behaviour before they are worth a
public API. `bb_facet_grid()` and the build/save workflow stay in later
releases.
